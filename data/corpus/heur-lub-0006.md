## Symptom
Slow temperature creep (1-3 degC/week) with rising high-frequency acoustic emission; friction-band energy up while discrete fault frequencies stay flat.
## Heuristic
HF energy rising without envelope peaks usually means lubrication, not bearing geometry. Regrease per schedule and observe: if HF energy drops >50% within a day, the diagnosis was starvation.
## Action window
Immediate regrease trial; if no response, escalate to bearing inspection.
## Pitfalls
Over-greasing causes the same temperature rise - check grease escape paths and quantities.
