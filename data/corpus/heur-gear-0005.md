## Symptom
Sidebands spaced at shaft speed around gear-mesh frequency (GMF) and harmonics.
## Heuristic
Healthy gearboxes show GMF with low, symmetric sidebands. Growing sideband families - especially asymmetric ones - indicate distributed wear or eccentricity; a strong 1x-spaced family around 2xGMF often precedes visible pitting.
## Action window
Trend sideband energy monthly; plan inspection when sideband-to-GMF ratio doubles from baseline.
## Pitfalls
Load changes move GMF amplitude a lot; compare like-for-like load conditions only.
