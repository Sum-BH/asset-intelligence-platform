## Symptom
High 1x running-speed amplitude (imbalance) vs high 2x with axial components (misalignment).
## Heuristic
Radial 1x dominant and stable with speed-squared scaling suggests imbalance. 2x exceeding 50% of 1x, plus axial vibration above half of radial, suggests coupling misalignment.
## Action window
Imbalance: schedule balancing at next planned stop. Misalignment: correct within 2-4 weeks - it accelerates bearing and seal wear.
## Pitfalls
Soft foot mimics both; check foot bolts with a dial indicator before condemning the coupling.
