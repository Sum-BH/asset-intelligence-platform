## Scope
Broadband vibration evaluation on non-rotating parts (velocity RMS, typically 10-1000 Hz), machine groups by power and mounting.
## Zones (paraphrased)
Zone A: new-machine quality. Zone B: acceptable for unrestricted long-term operation. Zone C: unsatisfactory for continuous operation - plan remedial action. Zone D: vibration severe enough to cause damage.
## Practical use
Use zone boundaries as escalation triggers in dashboards; combine with spectral diagnosis before acting - severity says HOW BAD, spectra say WHY.
